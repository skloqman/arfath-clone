Hajj

Hajj, stop by stop.

Hajj moves between Mina, Arafat and Muzdalifah in a fixed order. Arfath plans stays, transport and meals around it so the pilgrim can concentrate on worship. Choose a stop to follow the sun.

Hotels in Makkah and Madinah are chosen as close to the Haram as the package allows. Ask the team for the exact walking distance before you confirm.

Included in your Hajj

Comfort is the whole point.

  • Close stays

    Accommodation closest to the places of worship in Makkah, Madinah, Mina and Arafat, depending on the package you choose.

  • Good food

    A variety of dishes through the pilgrimage, including in Mina and Arafat, so you stay nourished while you perform the rites.

  • Easy transport

    Comfortable buses with wide seats between the points of the pilgrimage.

  • A team on the ground

    Experienced staff in India and in Saudi Arabia, with a guide present for the group during the journey.

  • Tarbiyati Program

    Training before departure so first-time pilgrims know the rites and what to expect.

عمرة

Umrah

The minor pilgrimage, done well.

Umrah is the journey to the holy Kaaba in Makkah. It is known as the minor pilgrimage, while Hajj is the major one and a pillar of Islam. It can be performed in four rites, in this order.

Your Umrah package

Close to the Haram, close to ease.

  • Hotels

    Stays in Makkah and Madinah a short distance from the Haram, as pilgrims on the 24 October group reported.

  • Meals

    Well-managed food arrangements throughout the trip.

  • Visa & flights

    Documentation, Saudi visa and air tickets handled by one office.

  • Group departures

    Travel with a group and a team that stays in touch from Hyderabad to Saudi Arabia.

About Arfath

Serving pilgrims since 1997.

Our legacy of helping Muslims perform Hajj and Umrah goes back over two decades.

Arfath Tours was founded in 1997. Every year since, the team has helped thousands of pilgrims fulfil their promise to Allah. Our professionals in India and Saudi Arabia work so that every part of the pilgrimage is carried out smoothly and the pilgrim stays relaxed.
